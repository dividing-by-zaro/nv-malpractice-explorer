Analysis

In-depth exploration of Nevada medical malpractice data

Over 30% of physicians have been sued at some point in their careers. Yet, of claims that have been filed, 65% are ultimately dropped, and contested claims (those which go to trial) go for the defendant 89% of the time. The claims published by the state of Nevada represent only those which are found to be substantiated. By examining these claims, we can better understand the landscape of healthcare in the state, and how to be both better doctors and better patients.

Cases by Specialty

Which medical specialties face the most malpractice complaints in Nevada?

The distribution of malpractice cases across medical specialties reveals patterns in healthcare risk. Specialties with higher patient volumes, more complex procedures, or greater diagnostic uncertainty tend to see more complaints.

Top Specialties by Case Count

Internal Medicine and Family Medicine practitioners appear frequently, in part because they represent large portions of the physician workforce and handle diverse patient conditions. Surgical specialties also feature prominently due to the inherent procedural risks involved.
